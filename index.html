CAPSTONE PROJECT

SewerGuard

An IoT-Based Sewer Water Blockage Detection System for Barangays in Cebu City

SewerGuard is our proposed capstone project that aims to improve sewer blockage monitoring and maintenance coordination in barangays in Cebu City. The project focuses on using Internet of Things (IoT) technology to help identify potential blockages and support more timely maintenance activities.

Internet of Things (IoT) Monitoring Community Sanitation
Learn More →

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Why SewerGuard?

The Problem

Sewer blockages can contribute to drainage problems and flooding. Depending only on manual inspections and community reports may delay the identification of issues that need attention.

Our Proposed Solution

SewerGuard proposes an IoT-based approach to sewer monitoring, with the goal of helping local communities identify possible blockage concerns and organize maintenance activities.

Project Goal

The goal is to support better sewer maintenance coordination and contribute to cleaner, safer communities through technology.
